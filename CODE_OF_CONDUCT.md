# Code of Conduct

We follow the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

In practice:
- Be respectful and assume good intent. Disagree with ideas, not people.
- Welcome newcomers; explain instead of dismissing.
- No harassment, discrimination, personal attacks or publishing others' private information.
- Do not post links to live scam sites in issues or discussions; give the domain as text.

Maintainers may remove comments, close discussions or block people who break these rules. To report a problem, contact the maintainer (GitHub: [@NurAbir](https://github.com/NurAbir)) privately.
