Before you start

  • Verity looks at web addresses only. It does not read page content, passwords or downloads.
  • Brand names and “real site” suggestions come from lists that can be incomplete or out of date.
  • It is not affiliated with any brand it protects, and it does not replace antivirus software or two-step verification.

Lists and updates

Verity can download the newest popular-site list, reported scam sites and public filter lists. It connects only to the sites listed under About, sends no cookies and nothing about your browsing, and only when you ask. Chrome asks your permission the first time.

    About and privacy

    Everything runs on your device. Verity sends nothing about the sites you visit to anyone. It contacts these sites only when you update lists: tranco-list.eu, phishunt.io, ublockorigin.github.io, curbengh.github.io and, if you turn on extra coverage, raw.githubusercontent.com.

    Data: Tranco list (tranco-list.eu); phishunt.io; uBlock Origin “Badware risks” (uAssets, GPL-3.0); Phishing URL Blocklist by curben (CC BY-SA 4.0), built from PhishTank, OpenPhish and phishunt; Phishing.Database (MIT). The licences of the upstream lists apply.
