Update lists

Verity can download the newest popular-site list (Tranco) and reported scam sites (phishunt). It connects only to those two sites, sends no cookies and nothing about your browsing, and only when you ask. Chrome will ask your permission the first time.

    About the data

    Everything runs on your device. Verity makes no network requests and sends nothing about the sites you visit to anyone.

    Popularity data: Tranco list (tranco-list.eu), which combines Cisco Umbrella, Majestic, Chrome UX Report and Cloudflare Radar data. Scam reports: phishunt.io (CC0). Attribution and licence terms of the upstream lists apply.
